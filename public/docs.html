Section 01 • Operational Overview

1. Executive Summary & Problem Context

Public municipal emergency triage systems across the BRICS+ alliance (India, Brazil, South Africa, Russia, China, and partner states) suffer from catastrophic friction during acute civic failures:

Vernacular Friction

Distressed citizens report crises in regional dialects (e.g., Telugu, Hindi, isiZulu, Portuguese, Marathi) with heavy colloquial code-switching. Traditional call centers miss urgent acoustic markers.

Degraded Rural Bandwidth

Severe storms, grid collapses, and monsoons degrade cellular connections to 2G/3G speeds. Standard image/video uploads time out, stalling civic response.

Queue Flooding & Duplication

A single 11kV live powerline failure generates hundreds of redundant calls in 10 minutes, exhausting emergency response capacity.

AI Triage Hallucinations

Unregulated generative models trigger expensive false alarms or downplay lethal risks without deterministic validation.

Section 06 • Triage Determinism

6. The 5-Level Hardcore Criticality Scoring Matrix

CYPHER replaces subjective human judgment with an immutable mathematical triage matrix. Level 5 dispatches are strictly barred unless explicit semantic or visual proof of un-isolated mortal hazard exists:

Level Classification Formal Threshold Criteria Target SLA Tactical Action
LEVEL 5 Critical Crisis Immediate, un-isolated threat to human life or systemic grid collapse (live 11kV cables in water, arterial bridge collapse, toxic gas leak). < 15 Mins Instant siren, automated grid trip, multi-unit tactical deployment.
LEVEL 4 Severe Emergency Major structural failure requiring engineering containment (sinkholes across road lanes, main 36" water trunk rupture, neighborhood blackout). < 2 Hours Heavy equipment mobilization, traffic perimeter lockdown.
LEVEL 3 Moderate Hazard Substantial localized disruption with low mortality risk (blocked arterial drainage during rain, broken local water lines). < 6 Hours Dispatched within current operational shift.
LEVEL 2 Standard Maintenance Localized non-hazardous wear (isolated potholes, burnt streetlight fixtures). < 24 Hours Batch routed to municipal contractor backlog.
LEVEL 1 Cosmetic / Minor Non-urgent aesthetic maintenance (graffiti, faded road lane markings). < 72 Hours Low-priority neighborhood maintenance pool.
